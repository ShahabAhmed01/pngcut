Privacy Policy

The short version: your selected media is processed on your device and is not uploaded for background removal.

Last updated: September 2026

Your media

PNGCut runs the entire background-removal pipeline — decoding, AI segmentation and export — inside your browser. Images and videos are read from your device, processed in memory, and saved back to your device. They are not uploaded to PNGCut or to any server controlled by PNGCut for background removal.

What PNGCut does not collect

  • No account, email or personal information — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No cookies used by PNGCut for tracking.
  • No images or videos — the selected media never leaves your browser.

Analytics

PNGCut uses Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to measure anonymized usage and performance. These tools do not use cookies and cannot identify you personally. They record anonymous page views, device type, country-level location, and Web Vitals metrics. No image or video content is included in these requests.

What your browser downloads and stores

On first use, the application downloads its AI segmentation model and its runtime (ONNX Runtime Web) from a public CDN. These are cached by your browser so later runs are faster and can work offline. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. These downloads do not contain your media.

Third-party requests

The only external requests PNGCut makes are for the application itself (served by the hosting provider) and the one-time download of the model/runtime assets from a CDN. No file contents are included in these requests.

What infrastructure providers inevitably see

Like any website, the hosting/CDN provider receives normal HTTP request metadata (such as your IP address and the requested URL) when the page and its assets load. PNGCut does not add additional tracking, but this network metadata is outside PNGCut's control. We describe this precisely rather than claiming that “nothing” travels over the network.

Metadata in output files

Exported images are re-encoded by your browser and do not carry the source file's metadata (such as EXIF location data) through the pipeline. If you need a specific metadata behavior, process a copy and verify the result.

Your responsibility

Because processing is local, you remain responsible for the files you choose and where you save them. PNGCut cannot access, recover or delete files on your device.

Reporting a privacy or security issue

See SECURITY.md for how to report issues privately, or reach us via Contact.
