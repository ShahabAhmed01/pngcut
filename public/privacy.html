Privacy Policy

Short version: we can't see your files, because they never leave your device.

Your files

PNGCut runs the entire background-removal pipeline — decoding, AI segmentation and export — inside your browser. Images and videos are read from your device, processed in memory, and saved back to your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server we control.

What we don't collect

  • No account or personal information — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, telemetry or ads.
  • No cookies for tracking.
  • No images or videos, ever.

What your browser stores

On first use, the AI model (a few tens of MB) is downloaded from a public CDN and cached in your browser's local storage so subsequent runs are fast and work offline. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Third-party requests

The only external requests PNGCut makes are the one-time downloads of the open-source segmentation model and the ONNX runtime from a public CDN. No file contents are included in these requests.
