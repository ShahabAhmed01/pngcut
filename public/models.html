AI models & cutout quality

PNGCut runs a real segmentation network on your own device. Here is exactly which model it uses, how that compares with the best models available today, and why the license decides what a free MIT app can ship.

Last reviewed: 15 September 2026

What PNGCut runs today

ISNet — the general-use variant of IS-Net, from Highly Accurate Dichotomous Image Segmentation (DIS, ECCV 2022). It runs through @imgly/background-removal on ONNX Runtime Web: WebGPU when your browser exposes an adapter, multi-threaded WASM otherwise.

Three weight tiers ship in the toolbar. They are the same architecture at different precisions, so the quality difference is subtle — the real trade-off is download size and speed.

Why PNGCut ships ISNet

  • The license works for a free app. ISNet is Apache-2.0, so an MIT project can ship the weights to your browser and you can fork it. RMBG-1.4 and RMBG-2.0 are source-available for non-commercial use only, and RVM is GPL-3.0 — none of those can be redistributed inside an MIT app.
  • It is the same family as the most popular model. BRIA built RMBG-1.4 by training IS-Net on a proprietary dataset, so much of what people call RMBG quality is ISNet plus a better training set. That makes a tuned ISNet a sensible baseline rather than a compromise.
  • It fits in a browser. The FP16 and 8-bit tiers keep the one-time download in the tens of megabytes, and inference still runs when WebGPU is missing (CPU or WASM).

How quality is pushed beyond the raw model

The model output is only half of a good cutout. Everything below runs locally, deterministically, with no server involved:

  • Mask refinement — speckle removal, edge-aware smoothing, halo-crushing contrast and an optional crisp threshold clean the raw alpha before you see it (Auto, Crisp, Soft or Off).
  • Device-aware tier selection — Auto picks a tier from a real WebGPU adapter probe rather than a user-agent guess, and a failed GPU session falls back to CPU instead of failing.
  • Bounded working size — very large images are processed at a bounded long side, preserving detail without exhausting browser memory.
  • A manual finish — erase and restore brushes, feathering and a compare slider for the parts no model gets right.
