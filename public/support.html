Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?
Yes — no account, no watermark, no usage quota. The AI runs in your browser, so there are no server costs to pass on to you. Processing is limited only by your device and browser.

Are my files uploaded?
No. Your selected media is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded for background removal. The app does download its model/runtime assets from a CDN; those requests do not contain your media.

Which image formats work?
PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP, depending on what your browser can decode. Export as transparent PNG or WebP, or JPEG with a background. SVG is not supported. Animated GIF is treated as a still image (first frame).

Which video formats work?
MP4 and WebM that your browser can play. Export is WebM (output capped at 1920 px long side) with your chosen background and, when supported, the original audio. Video is processed frame-by-frame, so short clips work best.

How does bulk removal work?
Click “Bulk removal” to pick many images, or “Select folder” to process every image in a folder (up to 300 per run). Download results individually or all at once.

Can I fix imperfect edges?
Yes. Use the Erase and Restore brushes (keyboard: B/R), adjust feathering, and compare with the original (C).

Troubleshooting

First run is slow.
The AI model (~40–80 MB) is downloaded once and cached. Later runs are much faster and can work offline.

Poor quality / slow on big images.
Chrome, Edge or Brave give the best results (WebGPU acceleration). On browsers without WebGPU it falls back to CPU (WASM), which is slower.

Video render failed.
Try a shorter clip, lower quality or fps, or another browser. Very long or high-resolution videos can exceed browser memory.
