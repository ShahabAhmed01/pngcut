Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?
Yes — unlimited, no account, no watermark. The AI runs in your browser, so there are no server costs to pass on to you.

Are my files uploaded?
No. Everything is processed locally. Your files never leave your device.

Which image formats work?
PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP. Export as transparent PNG or WebP, or JPEG with a background.

Which video formats work?
MP4 and WebM (anything your browser can play). Export is WebM with your chosen background and the original audio. Video is processed frame-by-frame, so short clips work best.

How does bulk removal work?
Click “Bulk removal” to pick many images, or “Select folder” to process every image in a folder (up to 300 per run). Download results individually or all at once.

Can I fix imperfect edges?
Yes. Use the Erase and Restore brushes (keyboard: B/R), adjust feathering, and compare with the original (C).

Troubleshooting

First run is slow.
The AI model (~40–80 MB) is downloaded once and cached. Later runs are much faster and work offline.

Poor quality / slow on big images.
Chrome, Edge or Brave give the best results (WebGPU acceleration). On Firefox it falls back to WASM, which is slower.

Video render failed.
Try a shorter clip, lower quality or fps, or another browser. Very long or 4K videos can exceed browser memory.

Still stuck?
Open an issue on GitHub.
