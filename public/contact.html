GitHub (preferred)

Open an issue on the issue tracker. Include your browser, operating system and what you tried — screenshots help.

Before filing a bug

  • Check Support — many questions are answered there.
  • Try a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or Firefox for the best WebGPU performance.
  • If video processing is slow, lower the quality/fps settings or try a shorter clip.
