Frequently asked questions

Is PNGCut really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no usage quota. Because the AI model runs inside your browser, processing is limited only by your device and browser.

Are my images or videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your selected media is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded for background removal. The app does download its AI model/runtime assets from a CDN; those requests do not contain your image or video.

Which formats are supported?

Images: PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP — exported as transparent PNG, WebP, AVIF (where your browser can encode it) or JPEG. Videos: MP4 and WebM — exported as WebM with your chosen background. Actual playback and export depend on what your browser supports.

Can I remove backgrounds from many files at once?

Yes. Use Bulk Removal to pick multiple images, or Select Folder to process every image in a folder. Download every result as a single ZIP, or one by one.

Does PNGCut work offline?

Yes, after the first visit. PNGCut is installable and caches its app files and AI model in your browser, so background removal keeps working with no connection.
